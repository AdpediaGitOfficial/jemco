06. Light glass surfaces

Translucency should be subtle and never reduce text contrast.
Glassmorphism
Glass card
A softer surface
Use a translucent white layer, a fine border and a restrained shadow.
Recommended glass recipe
Backgroundrgba(255,255,255,.72)
Borderrgba(255,255,255,.92)
Backdrop blur18px
Shadow0 8px 32px / 7%
Corner radius18–24px

Use glass for navigation, overlays, filters and selected cards. Keep core reading areas mostly solid white.

07. Bottom navigation

Four equal-width destinations with centered icons and labels.
Mobile pattern

Tap a destination to preview the active state. On mobile, reserve space below page content for the fixed navigation and iOS safe area.
